What does "worth acting on" mean?

It is what you stand to save by stepping in, minus what stepping in costs. Chasing a carrier or expediting a shipment is not free, so an order is only worth the trouble when the money you would protect is bigger than the trouble. Sorting on risk alone would put orders at the top that cost more to rescue than they are worth.
