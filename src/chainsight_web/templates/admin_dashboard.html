Why does almost nothing reach "critical"?

Because of what is in this catalogue. Every order in it is worth under 500, and the fixed goodwill cost of a late delivery is the larger half of the damage on a typical one — so a typical order simply cannot expose enough money to clear the critical band, however risky it is. You would see criticals in a business with a wider price range.

Why does the chart go all the way to 100% when the bars are so similar?
