Why can't I just choose their password?

Because then you would know it afterwards, and everything that account did could be blamed on you having signed in as them. A password that works once and must be replaced does not have that problem.

How was the very first administrator created?

On the server, with python -m chainsight_web init --email you@example.com — the one account that has to be made that way, because there was nobody yet to grant it.
