Why this is not simply the riskiest order first

Ranking on probability alone treats a rupee of margin and sixty as the same problem. Ranking on net benefit does not. On this dataset the effect is real but bounded: every order falls between a few rupees and 499.95, so the fixed goodwill penalty is the larger half of the exposure on a typical order and a typical order can never reach critical however risky it is. That is a property of the data rather than a bug, and it is stated here rather than implied away.
