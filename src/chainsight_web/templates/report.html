Why isn't the riskiest order simply the most urgent one?

Because ranking on risk alone treats one rupee of profit and sixty as the same problem. Ranking on what is worth acting on does not. On this particular dataset the effect is real but limited: every order is worth somewhere between a few rupees and 499.95, so the fixed goodwill cost of a late delivery is the larger half of the damage on a typical order — which also means a typical order here can never reach critical, however risky it is. That is a fact about the data rather than a bug, and it is said out loud here rather than quietly designed around.
